The beach

A crescent of white sand tucked between giant granite boulders, with Little Lion's Head standing guard behind. No shops, no streetlights — just the beach.

The water

That famous turquoise is pure Atlantic — bracing, to put it kindly. Brave the plunge and you've earned your spot on the sand for the rest of the day.

The sunsets

Llandudno faces due west, and by mid-November the sun doesn't drop into the sea until well after half past seven. Every single evening is a show.
